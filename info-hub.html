PPS Number Guide

You need a PPSN to work legally in Ireland

  1. Book an appointment at your local Intreo Centre or DSP office
  2. Bring your passport (or national ID for EU citizens)
  3. Bring proof of address (utility bill, bank letter, lease)
  4. Bring proof of why you need a PPSN (employment offer letter)
  5. Processing takes 1–5 working days

Working in Ireland

🇪🇺 EU / EEA Citizens

No visa or permit needed. You have the right to live and work freely in Ireland.

🌍 Non-EU Citizens

A work permit is required. The most common for skilled chefs is the Critical Skills Employment Permit (salary ≥ €32,000/yr).

🇧🇷 Brazilian Citizens

Ireland and Brazil have a Working Holiday Agreement. Brazilians aged 18–35 can apply for a 1-year working holiday visa.
